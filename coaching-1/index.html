FAQ

Questions

Do I need previous training experience?

No. If you're a beginner we start from your current ability. You can send videos of your exercises and I'll guide you step by step until you're confident on your own.

Do I need a gym?

No. Plans are built for a commercial gym, a home gym, minimal equipment, outdoors or travel. Several results above were done at home.

Is nutrition included?

Yes. It's built around your schedule, your work and the food you like, and you learn why, so you can keep eating well after the 12 weeks.

How does WhatsApp coaching work?

You message me directly with questions, videos, problems or schedule changes, and I answer with the reasoning, not just yes or no. Daily in the first 14 days.

Is this only for bodybuilding?

No. Bodybuilding is part of the process, not the goal. The goal is a body that looks powerful, moves well and performs.
